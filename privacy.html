Privacy Policy – Smart Ad Blocker

Developer: RK Solutions · Contact: rksolutions.offical@gmail.com · Effective: 16 November 2026

1. How the app works

Smart Ad Blocker uses Android's VPN feature only on your device. It checks the website names (DNS lookups) that apps ask for and blocks known ad and tracker names. It is not a real VPN: your internet traffic is not sent to our servers or any other VPN server. Allowed lookups go to the DNS provider you choose in Settings (Cloudflare by default).

2. Data that stays on your phone

  • Activity log (website name, app, time, blocked or allowed): last 500 entries, up to 7 days. You can turn it off or clear it at any time.
  • Your settings, allowed apps, allowed and blocked sites, and statistics.
  • The list of installed apps, used only to show the Apps screen.

We never collect or upload this data.

3. Advertising (Start.io)

The free version shows ads from Start.io. The Start.io SDK may collect your advertising ID, device information, IP address and approximate location to show and measure ads. Users in the EEA and UK are asked for consent first. See Start.io's privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Premium removes all ads.

4. Premium licence

If you buy Premium, we receive your device ID (to create a licence key that works on your phone) and the payment details needed to confirm your payment. We use them only to issue and check your licence.

5. Accessibility service (optional)

The optional status-bar logo uses an Android accessibility service only to find the position of status-bar icons so it can draw the logo next to them. It does not read, collect or share any content from your screen or other apps. It is off until you turn it on.

6. Optional system settings

If you use the optional "Hide the key icon" setup, the app changes one Android display setting (which status-bar icons are hidden) and restores it when protection stops or when you use "Uninstall Smart Ad Blocker" in Settings.

7. Updates and settings files

The app downloads blocklists, a settings file and update information from public servers. These requests do not include personal data.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

9. Your choices and deletion

You can clear the log, reset statistics or uninstall the app to remove all local data. To delete Premium licence data, email rksolutions.offical@gmail.com with your device ID.

10. Changes

We will update this page if the app's data use changes.
